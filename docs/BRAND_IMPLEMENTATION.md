* **Arabic Calligraphic Mark**: ستاركيت

### 3. Typography Hierarchy
* **Primary Latin Display & Body**: `Afacad Flux` (Weights: 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900)
  - Headers: Uppercase, tracking `0.12em` to `0.18em`, confident scale.
  - Body: High legibility, clean geometric sans-serif modernism.
* **Bengali Typography**: `Hind Siliguri` (Weights: 400, 500, 600, 700)
  - Native Bengali translations for every hoodie storyline and artisan accessory.
  - Native numbers and currency presentation (`৳` / BDT).
* **Arabic Brand Mark**: `Amiri` (Weights: 400, 700)
  - Classical Arabic calligraphic letterforms rendering the authentic mark `ستاركيت`.

---

### 4. Official Logo & Wordmark Rules
* The official StarkiT logo incorporates the **Arabic calligraphic insignia (ستاركيت)** positioned above or beside the spaced Latin **STARKIT** wordmark.
* **Clear Space Rule**: Maintain minimum clear space equal to 50% of the logo height around all perimeters.
* **Contrast Protection**: On dark backgrounds (`#170000`), the logo renders in Pure White with Champagne Gold Arabic accents. On light panels, the logo renders in Jet Black.
* **Prohibited Modifications**:
  - Never replace the logo with a generic star icon or clip art.
  - Never distort, stretch, or alter the kerning of the Latin letterforms.
  - Never crop or omit the Arabic insignia in official brand headers.

### 5. Product & Editorial Creative Direction
* **Streetwear Editorial Feel**: High-contrast, cinematic typography and full-bleed image presentation.
* **Spatial 3D Showroom**: Polyhedral lacquer monolith (`#140102`) suspended within an interactive champagne gold filigree cage, enveloped by dual orbital rings and a 500-particle cosmic nebula.
* **Tactile Micro-Interactions**:
  - Triple-layer glassmorphism (`backdrop-filter: blur(28px) saturate(200%)`).
  - Dynamic 3D card tilt with cursor-following specular radial glow.
  - Magnetic spring physics on interaction controls.
  - Scroll-linked reading progress indicator.
  - Dual-angle garment hover transitions (seamless cross-fade between front view and back graphic).
